# TikTok Monetization

TikTok has four main revenue streams: advertising, TikTok Shop, brand partnerships, and virtual gifts. Advertising is the largest stream, driven by in-feed and TopView ads. TikTok Shop has seen the highest growth, monetizing through seller commissions and advertising fees. Brand partnerships involve high-value deals like hashtag challenges and takeovers.

## Key points

- TikTok has four main revenue streams, with advertising being the largest. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Advertising primarily through in-feed ads that appear every 2-3 videos in the For You Page, and TopView ads that take over the screen when users first open the app. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- TikTok Shop has seen the highest growth over the past two years. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- TikTok Shop monetizes in two ways: taking a commission on every transaction, typically 2-8% depending on category, and charging sellers to advertise their products on the platform. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Brand partnerships are premium, high-value deals like branded hashtag challenges where brands pay $150K-500K+, brand takeover days for exclusive daily placement, and large-scale brand awareness campaigns. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Virtual gifts involve users sending gifts like roses or galaxies to creators during LIVE streams, and TikTok takes a cut of those transactions before paying out creators. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- TikTok Shop's growth shows they're successfully diversifying beyond pure advertising into commerce. ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
