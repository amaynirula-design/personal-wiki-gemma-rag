# Tanium Endpoint Management

Tanium is an endpoint management and security platform offering real-time visibility and control over all devices connected to a network. It distinguishes itself through a linear chain architecture that allows querying all endpoints in under 15 seconds, and a unified platform covering security, operations, patching, compliance, and more.

## Key points

- Tanium is an endpoint management and security platform that gives IT departments real-time visibility and control over all endpoints - laptops, servers, smartphones, and any other devices connected to the company's network ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Ability to query all endpoints in under 15 seconds, thanks to the linear chain architecture, which dramatically reduces server load ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Unified platform of modules that cover the full lifecycle: security, operations, patching, compliance, incident response, asset discovery, automation, and more ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tools like Tanium Guardian provide proactive vulnerability monitoring, while Tanium Automate lets teams execute playbooks and workflows that simplify everything from patching to onboarding new devices ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium consolidates multiple solutions for asset management, patching, EDR, compliance, and configuration into one platform ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Many enterprises think of Tanium as the backbone of their IT and security operations — real-time, accurate, and massively scalable ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
