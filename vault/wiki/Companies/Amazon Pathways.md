# Amazon Pathways

This note covers the motivation for working at Amazon and the appeal of the Pathways program. It highlights the desire to be actively involved in operational excellence and learn by doing on the floor, rather than observing from a distance.

## Key points

- Amazon built the most sophisticated delivery and logistics network in the world. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Amazon defends its operational advantage against competitors like Walmart, Target, and Tiktok Shop. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Operational excellence at a massive scale is what sets Amazon apart besides technology. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Great operations come down to always putting the customer first and delivering on commitments. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Pathways offers the chance to be on the floor of a fulfillment center and improve delivery processes. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- The owner is effective on-site with superintendents and mechanics solving real-time problems. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Pathways is seen as the natural next step for someone who has worked in operations and managed complex projects. ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
