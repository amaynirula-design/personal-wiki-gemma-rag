# Supporting a Struggling Peer

This note details an experience where the owner observed a peer struggling with a technically demanding construction project at Otis. The owner stepped in to offer support by walking through technical constraints and acting as a bridge with stakeholders, which helped the peer gain confidence and the project succeed.

## Key points

- Peer assigned highly complex, technically demanding construction project despite not having the technical background typically required for that type of work ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Peer was set up to struggle due to manpower constraints rather than fit ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Goal became to help stabilize the project and support my peer without undermining her credibility ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Proactively reached out to her and offered support, positioning myself as a potential resource rather than stepping in as a “fixer” ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Helped her ramp up on the technical aspects of the project by walking through system constraints, installation sequencing, and common risk areas based on my prior experience ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Acted as a bridge by framing technical limitations in clear language so stakeholders had realistic expectations ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Peer gained confidence, credibility with stakeholders, and was able to lead the project successfully rather than being overwhelmed by it ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- Team avoided a potential failure that would have been incorrectly attributed to an individual rather than a resourcing mismatch ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
