# Supply Chain Delays

This note details a situation where supply chain disruptions increased material lead times and put multiple projects at risk of significant delays. The owner used a two-pronged approach leveraging factory flexibility and field mechanic overtime to keep projects on schedule and maintain customer satisfaction.

- Material lead times increased from 12 to 16 weeks ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Informed customers of bad news ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Worked with our factory to implement partial shipments ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Allowed mechanics to start assembly immediately with available parts ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
