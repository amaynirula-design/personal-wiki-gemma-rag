# Inherited Project Overload

This note details the owner's experience inheriting an overwhelming workload at Otis after a colleague resigned, resulting in suddenly managing over 15 additional projects on top of an existing $10M portfolio. The owner successfully maintained delivery timelines and customer satisfaction by proactively seeking help from senior PMs, prioritizing critical deadlines, and renegotiating client timelines.

## Key points

- Inherited 15+ additional projects on top of an existing $10M portfolio ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Went from 2 to 15 projects overnight ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Reached out to senior PMs and scheduled weekly office hours ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Prioritized the most critical deadlines and which customers needed the most attention ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Renegotiated timelines with some clients, being transparent about the staffing situation ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Successfully delivered on those existing projects and also add new ones to my portfolio ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Strengthened relationships with senior PMs, who became mentors ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])

## Where the sources differ

- The bullet drafts in the Amazon and TikTok preps say the owner went from 2 to 15 projects two weeks after joining, during COVID; the full answers in all three preps say 15+ additional projects on top of an existing $10M portfolio.
- Only the Tanium prep says the colleague who resigned was the owner's mentor/buddy.
