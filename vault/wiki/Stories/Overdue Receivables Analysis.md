# Overdue Receivables Analysis

This note details an analysis of a serious cash flow issue at the San Jose office concerning significantly overdue invoices. The analysis involved segmenting data by line of business, interviewing stakeholders to find root causes, and presenting three data-driven solutions that resulted in a 15% drop in AR.

## Key points

- $7 million in invoices that were unpaid & significantly overdue represented 33% of total AR ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]])
- One line of business accounted for 55% of the overdue AR: 'new construction' in the Amazon and Tanium preps, 'my line of business' in the TikTok prep ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- PM’s were not getting notified when invoices were sent out ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Regional collectors were spread too thin ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Liens were not being filed strategically ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Presented three solutions to leadership ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Saw a 15% drop in AR ([[raw/Amazon Interview.docx|Amazon prep]], [[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])

## Where the sources differ

- The Amazon prep says the $7 million was 33% of total AR; the Tanium and TikTok preps say 34%.
- The TikTok prep describes the invoices as 90 days overdue; the Amazon and Tanium preps say 'unpaid & significantly overdue'.
