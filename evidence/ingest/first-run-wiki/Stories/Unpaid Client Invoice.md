# Unpaid Client Invoice

This story details a situation where a major recurring customer refused to pay a $125,000 invoice, creating conflict between Sales and Finance. The owner analyzed the financial health, negotiated a compromise involving a one-time courtesy discount and deferring the remaining balance, which secured the payment while preserving the long-term relationship.

## Key points

- Customer refused to pay $125,000 that was owed ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Conflict between Sales and Finance regarding writing off the amount ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Analyzed internal system and discovered the project would remain net positive overall without the payment ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Proposed a compromise: write off half of the unpaid amount as a one time courtesy discount ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- The remaining balance would be deferred into a future project ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- This win-win solution prevented a potential financial loss and protected the long-term relationship ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]], [[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
