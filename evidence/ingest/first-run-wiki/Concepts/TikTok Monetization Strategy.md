# TikTok Monetization Strategy

TikTok monetizes through four main revenue streams: in-feed and top view ads, TikTok Shop commissions, brand partnerships, and virtual gifts to creators. The platform's strengths include algorithm superiority, a highly engaged Gen Z user base, and seamless TikTok Shop integration. Ad formats are categorized by funnel stage, with metrics varying from Impressions for awareness to ROAS for conversion.

## Key points

- TikTok has four main revenue streams: advertising, TikTok Shop, brand partnerships, and virtual gifts ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Advertising primarily through in-feed ads (every 2 to 3 videos) and TopView ads (when users first open the app) ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- TikTok Shop monetizes by taking a commission on every transaction, typically 2-8% depending on category, and charging sellers to advertise products ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Brand partnerships include hashtag challenges where brands pay $150K-500K+, brand takeover days, and large-scale brand awareness campaigns ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Algorithm superiority keeps users engaged longer and makes ads feel native to the app ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- TikTok is Gen Z dominant, spending around 95 mins on the app every day, driving high engagement rates ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Top of funnel metrics include Impressions, Reach, Frequency, and Brand Awareness Lift ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
- Middle of funnel metrics include Click-Through Rate, Time on Site, Email signups, and Add-to-Cart actions ([[raw/Tiktok Interview Prep.docx|Tiktok prep]])
