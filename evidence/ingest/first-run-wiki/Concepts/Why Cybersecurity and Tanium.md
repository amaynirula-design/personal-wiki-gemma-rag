# Why Cybersecurity and Tanium

This note covers the owner's interest in cybersecurity, sparked by a canceled flight, and an understanding of Tanium's role as an endpoint management and security platform. It details Tanium's key innovations, such as its ability to query endpoints quickly and its unified platform approach.

## Key points

- Interest in cybersecurity started with a canceled flight due to a CrowdStrike outage. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium is an endpoint management and security platform providing real-time visibility and control over endpoints like laptops, servers, and smartphones. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium's key innovation is querying all endpoints in under 15 seconds due to the linear chain architecture. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium offers a unified platform covering security, operations, patching, compliance, incident response, asset discovery, and automation. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tools like Tanium Guardian provide proactive vulnerability monitoring. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium Automate lets teams execute playbooks and workflows to simplify tasks like patching and onboarding new devices. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
- Tanium consolidates multiple solutions for asset management, patching, EDR, compliance, and configuration into one platform. ([[raw/Tanium Interview Prep.docx|Tanium prep]])
